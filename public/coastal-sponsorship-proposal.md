# SPONSORSHIP PROPOSAL
## YOUTH ATHLETIC & COMMUNITY PARTNERSHIP

**Prepared For:** Coastal Community Church  
**Prepared By:** Delray Beach Rocks Youth Football Organization (8U Division)  
**Partnership Level:** Community Championship Partner ($1,000)  
**Focus Area:** Player Uniforms & Custom Gear Bags  

---

### Executive Summary

The Delray Beach Rocks and Coastal Community Church are proud to propose a collaborative partnership aimed at empowering local youth through sports, mentoring, and community building. This proposal outlines a $1,000 Community Championship Sponsorship package designed to fully fund custom team uniforms and specialized gear bags for the 8U youth football team, while establishing a direct pathway for local families to connect with Coastal Community Church.

---

### The Vision: Why We Partner

Youth sports provide a critical foundation for character, discipline, and teamwork. However, the cost of high-quality equipment and uniforms can be a significant barrier for local families. 

By sponsoring the Delray Beach Rocks 8U division, Coastal Community Church acts as a direct catalyst for positive community change. This partnership is more than a financial contribution; it is an active bridge connecting church members, families, and young athletes in a supportive network.

---

### The $1,000 Sponsorship Package Breakdown

#### 1. Custom Team Uniforms
* Funding covers high-quality home and away jerseys for the 8U roster.
* Jerseys will feature the Coastal Community Church brand mark/logo prominently on the sleeve or shoulder area.

#### 2. Specialized Athletic Gear Bags
* Funding covers durable, custom gear bags for each player to carry their helmets, pads, and equipment.
* Bags will be custom-printed with the Delray Beach Rocks insignia alongside the Coastal Community Church logo.

#### 3. Field Banner Placement
* A custom-designed 3'x6' vinyl banner featuring Coastal Community Church will be displayed at all Delray Beach Rocks home games, practices, and league events.

#### 4. Digital & Social Media Recognition
* Prominent logo placement on the official Delray Beach Rocks partnership section.
* Monthly sponsor spotlights across the organization's social media platforms and email newsletters.

---

### Interactive Family Engagement Program

To create a tangible connection between the team and the church, we have launched a dedicated digital RSVPs campaign via:
**signup.f1rstposition.com**

#### How the Engagement Campaign Works:
1. **Parent Registrations**: Parents of the 8U division register their families to attend a Sunday service or a community outreach event (such as the West Boca Campus Block Party).
2. **Attendance Check-In**: The coaching staff tracks Sunday attendance through a digital portal.
3. **Milestone Unlocking**: As families check in and visit Coastal Community Church, the sponsorship milestones are unlocked, showing active community integration.

### Agreement & Next Steps

To move forward with this partnership:
1. **Review and Confirm**: Confirm acceptance of the Community Championship Partnership terms.
2. **Logo Asset Provision**: Provide high-resolution vector files of the Coastal Community Church logo for jersey and banner printing.
3. **Funding Transfer**: Coordinate the $1,000 sponsorship contribution to the Delray Beach Rocks Youth Football Organization.

For questions or to coordinate details, please contact the Delray Beach Rocks coaching staff. Thank you for your continued dedication to supporting local youth and building a stronger community together.
